# dowiz × Dubin & Sushi — 50-second promo: plan and shot-by-shot script

Version 2 · 2026-09-19 · English master (UK/SQ title variants in §7) · assembly in OpenMontage / Remotion, B-roll via Higgsfield.
Dubin & Sushi has given full permission for its name, mark, storefront and kitchen footage.

## 1. What we show, and to whom

**Viewer:** the owner of a small pizzeria, bakery, sushi bar or craft food producer, who pays an
aggregator a cut of every order and lives inside someone else's catalogue.

**Two promises, each shown rather than stated:**
1. **No per-order fees. No tariffs. No commission.** One flat subscription; every order's profit
   stays with the venue. The subscription amount is NEVER shown in the film: the message is the
   absence of fees, not a price.
2. **Your own mini-app.** A branded storefront on the venue's own subdomain, in its colours, with
   its seal and mark, installable on the home screen, with live tracking.

**Example client:** Dubin & Sushi, Durrës. Real surfaces, captured from the screen:
- storefront `https://sushi-durres.dowiz.org` (night garden: enso, branch with leaves, seal ドウビン, gold foil, 165 dishes with photos);
- owner console `https://sushi-durres.dowiz.org/admin/` (orders with live ETA, menu, stock, couriers, integrations);
- courier app `https://dubin-sushi.dowiz.org/courier/` (full-screen map, one job at a time, swipe to deliver);
- tracking page with the ink ocean and the way-home map (venue → courier → door).

**Formats:** 9:16 (Reels / TikTok / Shorts, primary), 1:1 (feed), 16:9 (site / YouTube). One timeline,
three crops; UI is captured on a phone viewport (390×844 at DPR 3), so 9:16 is native.

## 2. Tone, look, rhythm

- **Look:** premium minimalism with a craft texture. Palette = dowiz + Dubin & Sushi: paper `#0b1717`,
  gold `#c9a35a` as brushed foil (the storefront's own gradient), warm `#e0754d`, sage `#8a9a7b`.
  Type: serif display for headlines (as in the storefront), mono for numbers.
- **Rhythm:** two tempos. The first 8 seconds, the pain, cut hard (0.6–0.9 s shots). From second 8,
  the answer, the tempo settles (1.5–2.5 s shots) and UI moves the way the app itself moves.
  The drama is the contrast: money burning → one calm gold `0`.
- **Language:** English master; UK and SQ title tracks share the timeline (§7).

Total 50 s. **30-second cut:** shots 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12, 15 (3–4 s each; the four advantages
compressed into one list title on shot 10: "one hub · autoposting · post-quantum security · local AI").
**15-second pre-roll:** 2, 4, 7, 15.

**Why the four advantages are worded this way (each verified in the code, 2026-09-19):**
- *One hub for every order* — storefront, phone orders, inbound WhatsApp and Instagram (one Meta
  webhook), partner API keys and MCP agents all land in one order log; the courier app and the live
  ETA serve them all the same way. Aggregators (Wolt, Glovo) are NOT connected yet, so the film
  does not name them.
- *Autoposting* — the hub drafts posts about real menu changes (a new dish, one back on sale, the
  week's most ordered) and publishes to the Telegram channel and Instagram only after the owner
  approves. "On your approval" stays on screen: it is honest, and it is the advantage.
- *Post-quantum security* — the kernel carries real ML-DSA-65 signatures and a hybrid
  X25519 + ML-KEM-768 key exchange, checked byte-for-byte against NIST test vectors, with no
  classical-only fallback. The algorithm names on screen are exact, not decoration.
- *Local AI* — the assistant talks to a local model by default (Ollama on the venue's server) and,
  while switched off, sends nothing anywhere; the console shows "Answered by the model on this
  server". A cloud model is possible only by the owner's explicit choice.

## 4. Music and sound

**A hybrid, not either/or.**
- 0:00–0:08 (pain): near silence. Paper rustle, a muffled kitchen hum, one low hit on "burn".
  No music: silence before the hit works harder than a beat.
- 0:08 (flip to `0`): the beat enters. **Lo-fi with a Japanese tint**: soft kick/snare at 84–88 BPM,
  brushed percussion, a short koto or shamisen pluck in the answers, a warm pad. That is the craft
  feel and Dubin & Sushi at once; a tech-startup EDM drop would tear the storefront's aesthetic.
- 0:30–0:46 (hub, autoposting, security, local AI): add a top layer, a light arpeggio or hi-hat, so
  the pace reads as growth, not a jump.
- 0:48–0:50 (CTA): the beat stops on one gold chord with a reverb tail and the seal's stamp.
- UI sounds: quiet taps (the app's own pebble sound), a swoosh on a status flip, the loader's leaf
  rustle. All at −18…−22 dB under the voice.
- Voice: calm, confident, no hard sell. Neutral English; the UK and SQ versions are dubbed to the
  same timings.

## 5. Assets and how to get them

**Screen captures (real surfaces, no mockups):**
- Playwright with `recordVideo`, iPhone 14 Pro preset, 390×844, DPR 3, 60 fps: storefront (loader →
  grid → card → cart → address → payment), console (orders, ETA, More, Posts, Assistant,
  "Check all"), courier (sign-in → shift → active run → swipe).
- **This machine's limit:** headless Chromium does not render WebGL, so the ink ocean and the
  MapLibre maps come out blank in captures. Shots 9 and part of 10 are recorded on a real phone or
  in headed Chromium on a machine with a GPU. Everything else is captured here.
- The courier's active run needs a real free order on the `dubin-durres` hub (a test order,
  placed and then cancelled).
- The assistant shot needs the assistant switched on with a local endpoint, or a short stand-in
  answer for the capture.

**Generated (Higgsfield, image-to-video or text-to-video, 4–5 s, 24 fps, 9:16):**
- H1 "Gloved hands packing sushi rolls into a craft box, dark kitchen, warm rim light, steam,
  85 mm macro, shallow depth of field, slow push-in."
- H2 "Same scene: the lid closes, banknotes lift from under it and burn, ash, the light turns
  cold." (Alternative: generate the box only; add notes and ash as Remotion particles, which is
  easier to control.)
- H3 "A sushi chef lays a slice of salmon on rice, close-up on the hands, 120 fps slow motion,
  Japanese aesthetic, black ceramics, gold side light."
- H4 "A café owner picks a phone off a wooden shelf, looks at the screen, smiles, warm morning
  light, 35 mm." (The screen is replaced by tracking in Remotion.)

**Stills:** the Dubin & Sushi mark (`logoUrl` from the hub), the dowiz mark (gold `d`, as on the
sign-in screen), the seal ドウビン, the storefront's enso SVG, 6–8 dish photos at 3:2 from the
catalogue (never zoomed, exactly as the storefront shows them).

## 6. Assembly in OpenMontage / Remotion

Composition `DowizPromo50`, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 1500 frames. Sections as separate `<Sequence>`s.

- **Layers (bottom to top):** `Paper` (`#0b1717` with 4% grain), `Broll` (Higgsfield mp4 via `Video`),
  `Screen` (UI capture inside a phone frame, 44 px radius, shadow 0 30 80 rgba(0,0,0,.5)),
  `Text` (titles), `Grain/Vignette` (12% vignette).
- **Type:** titles `"Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Georgia, serif`, 64–88 px, tracking −1%;
  captions mono 22–26 px, uppercase, tracking 22%; the `0`: mono 220 px.
- **Colour:** text `#f1e8d8`, gold `#c9a35a`, "pain" red `#DC2626` (the product's own status red),
  success `#0D9488`.
- **Motion:** every title enters as in the storefront: `opacity 0→1`, `translateY 12px→0`,
  `blur 3→0`, 420 ms, easing `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`. The flip `−35% → 0`: rotateX 90° over
  260 ms with the colour swap at the midpoint. Foil on the `0`: animated `background-position` on
  the gold gradient, 6.4 s cycle (the product's `--spectral-speed`).
- **Transitions:** pain → answer is a hard cut on the hit. After that, 12-frame crossfades. Loader →
  grid has no transition: the capture is already animated.
- **Banknote particles (shot 2):** 18–24 note sprites from under the lid, rising with spin,
  `opacity` to 0 over 900 ms, an ash trail of 40 particles, `#DC2626` → grey.
- **Titles:** 9:16 safe area, no text below 220 px from the bottom or above 180 px from the top
  (social UI). CTA mark centred.
- **Subtitles:** on by default (feeds play muted), mono 28 px on `rgba(11,23,23,.72)`.
- **Export:** H.264 High, 12 Mbps, AAC 192 kbps; 1:1 and 16:9 as separate compositions on the same
  timeline with the `Screen` layer re-framed (phone centred, B-roll stretched).

Minimal OpenMontage skeleton (pseudo):

## 8. Decisions already made

1. **No amount on screen, ever.** The film says "no per-order fees, no tariffs, no commission"; the
   subscription is one flat fee and its figure belongs on the pricing page, not in a 50-second film.
2. **Dubin & Sushi:** full permission granted for the name, the mark, storefront captures and
   kitchen footage (generated H3 may show recognisable details).
3. **CTA:** `dowiz.org`.
4. **Language:** English master; UK and SQ dubs and subtitle tracks on the same timeline.

## 9. Production steps

1. Capture the UI with a Playwright script (`e2e/kit-regression/_promo_capture.mjs`, to be added):
   storefront, console (orders, posts, assistant, integrations), courier; shoot the ocean and the
   maps on a phone.
2. Generate H1–H4 in Higgsfield from the prompts in §5; keep one take each.
3. Build the skeleton from §6 in OpenMontage, replace placeholders, render a 9:16 draft.
4. Record the VO from §3, mix per §4, burn in subtitles.
5. Render three formats and three subtitle tracks.
