# Data processing agreement between the venue and dowiz

Version: dpa v1 2026-09-24

This agreement is made under Albania's Law No. 124/2024 "On Personal Data Protection", Art. 26, and, where it applies, Art. 28 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation. It applies between the venue that uses the dowiz ordering system ("the venue") and dowiz, the operator of the dowiz.org platform ("dowiz"). It is accepted by a click when the venue's hub is created, or in the owner console, and the accepted version is stored on the venue's record.

## 1. Roles

- The venue is the controller of the personal data of its customers, guests, staff and couriers.
- dowiz is the venue's processor for that data. It processes it only to run the venue's ordering, booking, kitchen, delivery and payment tools, and only on the venue's documented instructions. The settings the venue chooses in its console are those instructions.
- dowiz is a controller only for its own data: the accounts of venue owners and staff on the platform, and the platform's waiting list.

## 2. Subject, duration, nature and purpose

- Subject: running the venue's storefront, orders, bookings, kitchen tickets, deliveries, table service, customer messages, customer records the venue keeps, consent records, stored-value balances and backups.
- Duration: for as long as the venue uses dowiz, and then until the data is returned or deleted under section 9.
- Nature: storing, organising, displaying, sending to the recipients the venue switches on, backing up and erasing.

## 3. Categories of data and of people

- People: the venue's customers and guests, its staff, and the couriers it engages.
- Data: names, phone numbers, delivery addresses and map positions, what was ordered, notes, table bookings, messages, payment method and amounts, stored-value balances, consent choices, the venue's notes about a guest, staff identifiers, sign-in sessions, and the latest position of a courier on a delivery.
- dowiz does not ask for special categories of data. A guest's allergy note is kept only if the venue or the guest writes it.

## 4. dowiz's obligations

- Process only on the venue's instructions, and tell the venue at once if an instruction appears to break the law.
- Keep the data confidential: only people bound to confidentiality may access it.
- Keep the security measures in section 7.
- Help the venue answer its customers' requests (access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) within the 30 days of Law 124/2024 Art. 12(4). The console's customer tools and the erasure command exist for this.
- Tell the venue about a personal data breach immediately after becoming aware of it (Law 124/2024 Art. 29(2)), and in any case within 24 hours, with what is known, so the venue can notify the Commissioner within 72 hours (Art. 29(1)).
- Give the venue the information it needs to show compliance, and allow audits on reasonable notice.

## 5. Sub-processors

The venue gives dowiz general written authorisation to use these sub-processors (Law 124/2024 Art. 26(2)):

- Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting of the platform, the venue's database, file storage and email. Safeguard: Cloudflare's Customer Data Processing Addendum with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses; Cloudflare states that it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- Stripe: card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments, when card payments are enabled. Stripe acts under its own terms, which include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

dowiz will tell the venue at least 30 days before adding or replacing a sub-processor, by email and in the console, and the venue may object; if the objection cannot be resolved the venue may end this agreement.

## 6. Services the venue chooses itself

These receive data only when the venue switches them on, under the venue's own contract with them. dowiz only transmits what the venue has asked for:

- Telegram (kitchen chat): the ticket carries the customer's first name and initial, the order, the address, and a phone number only for a delivery.
- Meta (WhatsApp Business, Instagram): the conversations the venue holds with its customers.
- The venue's own S3-compatible storage: the nightly backup copies, which the venue can seal with its own key.
- The venue's AI assistant endpoint: order facts without any customer name or phone.
- ebills.al: read-only, with the venue's own login; no customer data is sent.

## 7. Security measures

- All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- Each venue's data lives in its own database object.
- Passwords are stored only as Argon2 hashes.
- Access to the console requires a signed-in owner or staff member, and each request is checked against that person's membership of the venue it acts on.
- Nightly backup copies can be sealed with the venue's key and are kept at most 22 days.
- An erasure is recorded in a pseudonymous erasure register, so restoring a backup applies it again.

## 8. Transfers outside Albania

Cloudflare and Stripe process data outside Albania. The transfers rely on the Commissioner's Decision no. 1 of 30 April 2025, which recognises countries with an EU adequacy decision (for the United States, organisations certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework), and on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses. Where neither applies, a transfer is made only when it is necessary to perform the customer's order (Law 124/2024 Art. 41(3)(b)).

## 9. End of the agreement

When the venue leaves dowiz, the owner can download a full copy of its data from the console (Backup). dowiz then deletes the venue's data within 30 days, except where a law requires it to be kept. Cloudflare's own recovery points expire 30 days later. The venue's copies in its own storage are the venue's.

## 10. Contact, law and changes

- dowiz privacy contact: privacy@dowiz.org.
- This agreement is governed by the law of the Republic of Albania. The supervisory authority is the Commissioner for the Right to Information and Personal Data Protection.
- A changed text gets a new version. The console shows the venue that a new version is waiting, and the venue accepts it there.
