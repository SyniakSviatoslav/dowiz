# Courier: the courier app

The courier app is at `/courier/` on the venue's address. It shows **one job at a time**, because
the person holding the phone is on a scooter. It follows the phone's light or dark theme and speaks
Albanian, English and Ukrainian.

## Getting in

1. The venue invites you from the console's **Couriers** tab and gives you a code. The code works
   once and lasts a week.
2. Open `/courier/` on the venue's address, tap **I have an invite code**, enter your phone, the code
   and a password of at least 8 characters. The venue never sees your password.
3. Next time, sign in with your email or phone and your password.

Your sign-in belongs to the venue whose address you opened. The same phone number at another venue
is a different account there.

## A shift

- **Start shift** to receive orders. Off shift, no orders come in.
- **Ready for pickup** lists the orders waiting for a courier. Pick one and tap **Take**. An order
  can also be **offered to you** by the venue; the offer shows how long is left to take it.
- **Pick up the order** at the venue, then you are **on the way**. **In maps** opens the address in
  your map app; **Call** rings the customer.
- **Delivered**: swipe to confirm. For a cash order the app asks how much cash you received and
  records any shortfall.
- **Refused at the door**: if the customer will not take the order, record it (with an optional
  note, for example "nobody opened the door"). No money was taken; the order ends through a refund
  on the venue's side.
- **End shift** when you are done.

**My shifts** and **History** show your runs, cash in hand and earnings. The box at the bottom lets
you ask about your own deliveries in plain words.

## No signal

Taps made without a connection are saved on the phone and sent by themselves when the connection
returns; the header shows how many are still unsent. Each saved tap carries a key, so a tap that
arrives twice is done once. If you open the app again underground, the current job is still on the
screen. If the order changed while you were offline, the app tells you the saved action was not
accepted.
