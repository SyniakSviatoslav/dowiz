# Free order SMS for a venue in Albania (and Ukraine)

Lane W-SMS, 2026-10-03/04. Operator ask (verbatim): "для sms потрібно знайти і добавити безкоштовний варіант й обов'язково усе перевірити".
Every fact below carries its source and the date it was read. "Search summary" means the fact came from a web-search
digest, not from the page itself (the fetcher refused several domains); treat those as lower confidence.

## Verdict

**Default: the venue's own Android phone, running "SMS Gateway for Android" (SMSGate, capcom6/android-sms-gateway,
Apache-2.0), in Cloud mode.** The Worker calls `POST https://api.sms-gate.app/3rdparty/v1/messages` with the
username/password the app shows. The SMS goes out from the venue's own SIM, so its cost is whatever the venue's
plan charges (in Albania and Ukraine most plans include an SMS bundle). dowiz pays nothing and holds no platform key.

The same seam takes an owner-supplied paid provider with the owner's own key (Twilio is wired; textbee is wired as
a second phone-gateway option). Like the venue-owned Telegram bot: **never a platform key.**

Why SMSGate over the others: the most permissive licence (httpSMS is AGPL), no monthly cap in the free cloud
(textbee caps 300/month, which a busy venue passes in a week), active releases, multi-SIM, a self-hostable
"private server" if the venue (or dowiz later) wants the relay off a third party, and optional end-to-end
encryption of text + recipients.

Facts that shape the build:
- **Cloud mode works from a Worker.** The Worker talks to `api.sms-gate.app`; the phone keeps its own connection.
  Local mode (`http://<phone-ip>:8080/message`) needs a public IP on the phone, which a venue does not have: not used.
- **The phone may be asleep up to ~15 min.** FAQ: "Due to the app's idle mode behavior, the device may only connect
  to the server once every 15 minutes." The app has a foreground service + wake lock and a "disable battery
  optimisations" switch; the owner must turn that on (setup step). Order-status SMS can therefore arrive late; the
  outbox does not retry a 202 (the cloud queued it).
- **Android's own limit.** AOSP `SmsUsageMonitor`: `DEFAULT_SMS_MAX_COUNT = 30` per `DEFAULT_SMS_CHECK_PERIOD = 60000`
  ms per app before the system asks the user to confirm (android.googlesource.com, frameworks/opt/telephony, main,
  read 2026-10-04). Our per-venue budget is far below that.
- **Carrier fair use.** The SIM sends person-to-person traffic; bulk sending from a consumer SIM can get the SIM
  throttled or blocked. The app has random delay, per-period limits and SIM rotation for that (FAQ, "operator
  restrictions"). Our default budget (60/day per venue) is order-status volume, not marketing.
- **Android 15+**: Play Protect may block the APK (not on Google Play); the SMS permission may be greyed out and must
  be granted by hand (FAQ, read 2026-10-03). Setup text tells the owner.
- **Sender** = the venue's own phone number. No alphanumeric sender name via a SIM (FAQ "custom sender name").
- **Sent texts are visible** in the phone's SMS app (FAQ). The phone is the venue's; that is acceptable and transparent.
- **Processor**: in Cloud mode the relay `api.sms-gate.app` sees the recipient number and the text unless E2E is on.
  Registry row added (processor). The text carries only the order number + venue name.

free tier found. Ukraine: TurboSMS, AlphaSMS etc. are paid, alpha-name approval ~2-4 weeks (search summary). Not wired;
the provider seam takes one later.

## 4. Legal

- **Albania**: Law No. 124/2024 on Personal Data Protection (GDPR-aligned, in force 1 Feb 2025) and the
  Commissioner's Instruction No. 4 of 30 Apr 2025 on direct marketing (search summaries of kpmg.com/al,
  karanovicpartners.com, idp.al, 2026-10-04). Order-status SMS is transactional, not marketing, but a phone number is
  personal data. We use **explicit opt-in**: an unticked box at checkout, recorded in the consent log with the order;
  no box, no SMS. Every SMS text ends with a short stop line ("STOP: reply or tell the venue"); the owner can switch
  SMS off per venue. No marketing content is ever sent on this rail.
- **Sender ID (Albania)**: alphanumeric sender IDs are supported for A2P with no pre-registration; two-way A2P is not
  supported (search summary of sent.dm Albania guide and Twilio/Vonage country pages, 2026-10-04). Irrelevant for the
  default path (the SIM's own number is the sender).
- **Ukraine**: alpha names need operator approval (2-4 weeks); a SIM sends from its number. Ukrainian data law
  (Law 2297-VI) requires consent for processing; the same opt-in covers it.

## 5. What the operator must do to make it LIVE-PROVEN

1. On an Android phone with a SIM (Albanian for +355 customers): install the SMSGate APK from
   `github.com/capcom6/android-sms-gateway/releases` (v1.77.0 or later), grant SMS permission, switch **Cloud server**
   on, tap **Online**, turn off battery optimisation in Settings → System.
2. Copy the Cloud username + password the app shows into the owner console: Settings → SMS → Gateway.
3. Tap **Test** with your own number; then run `node tools/live-proof/probes/feature-sms.mjs` with the QA hub env.
Without step 1-2 the probe prints NEEDS-KEY, never a pass.
