# dowiz v2026.09.0

The first release since `2026.07.0`. In these two months dowiz stopped being a kernel with a demo and
became a restaurant system running for a real venue, Dubin & Sushi in Durrës
(`sushi-durres.dowiz.org`): four apps for guests, the owner, the room and couriers, one Cloudflare
Worker, and one Durable Object per venue holding that venue's records. Everything below is in the
tree at this tag. Planned work is listed at the end and in
[`docs/design/ROADMAP-2026-09-22.md`](../design/ROADMAP-2026-09-22.md).

**Version.** CalVer, as `CHANGELOG.md` declares (`YYYY.MM.PATCH`): the last tag is `2026.07.0`, the
changelog's last entry `2026.07.3` was never tagged, and this is the first release of September, so
`2026.09.0`. The `v` prefix is what `.github/workflows/release.yml` listens for.

## Features

### For guests (storefront)
- The venue's own storefront on its own subdomain, in Albanian, English and Ukrainian, installable
  as an app; the venue's own mark, colours and 165 dishes.
- Delivery and pickup with one pricer for the preview and the checkout, a waiting time the kernel
  computes in whole minutes from the venue's own kitchen and queue, and live tracking on a map.
- Order from the table through a signed QR link per table; the round joins the table's open bill and
  waits for staff.
- Book a table without an account, with a share link that works on any phone.
- Marketing consent recorded with the exact wording shown, withdrawable at any time.
- Feedback on an order is a sentence, never a score. A stamp card exists and is off by default.

### For the owner (console)
- A phone-first console in three languages: orders with live waiting times, menu, stock, couriers,
  and a "More" screen of everything else.
- Refunds for orders past `PENDING`; a delivery refused at the door ends through the same path.
- Menu import from a spreadsheet with a preview first; allergens and options per dish.
- Stock: supplies, recipes, purchases, write-offs that name who signed them, a waste report, and a
  dish's cost as a weighted average of purchases, in integers.
- Staff: invite kitchen, waiter or counter-manager members, change a role, suspend.
- Bookings screen and a floor-plan editor; printable table QR codes.
- Customer cards that hold only what the orders cannot tell, masked contact details with a log of
  every reveal, two spellings of one phone linked without merging, and forgetting a person in place.
- Campaigns as approved WhatsApp templates to consented customers only, with consent re-checked when
  each message is sent.
- Exceptions report (voids, comps, refunds, till gaps) with a signer on every row; analytics in the
  venue's own time zone; a health pane with every store's size against its real ceiling.
- Integrations, each with a "prove it" check: the venue's own Telegram bot, WhatsApp and Instagram
  through one Meta webhook, a nightly copy to the venue's own S3 bucket, the venue's AI endpoint, a
  kitchen printer that fetches its own tickets, eBills import, and the venue as an MCP server.

### For the room (room app)
- The floor with six table states worked out from orders and bookings.
- Rounds, amend in intent form (add, remove, re-count, comp) with a stale edit refused rather than
  overwritten; move lines between rounds; move a sitting to another table.
- Split payments across as many payments as it takes, in EUR and ALL at a stated rate, with tips;
  over-payment refused; cash only into an open till.
- The till: open, blind count, close, pay in, pay out, per currency; tips per person.
- The amend and pay rules run in wasm in the browser, byte-identical to the server.

### For couriers (courier app)
- One job at a time: take, pick up, deliver with a cash handover, or record "refused at the door".
- Taps made without signal are saved and replayed with an idempotency key; the app reopens
  underground on the current job. A courier's sign-in belongs to the host's venue.

### eBills
- The venue's certified till sales are imported from ebills.al into the same order log (read
  direction only). A sender exists and is switched off (`SEND_ENABLED = false`): dowiz does not
  fiscalise sales.

## Fixes

- An order's source is a closed set written once, never taken from the request body.
- A courier's replayed tap gets the first call's answer instead of a refusal, and a retried order is
  not placed twice.
- Two pricers disagreed (the preview quoted what the checkout refused); there is one now.
- The venue's local midnight was invented in six places and was a summer constant; analytics, hours
  and reports now use the venue's time zone.
- The order log refused an order with 56 cells free; append logs now grow.
- A truncated store file was read as the version before it; it is refused now.
- A collected order could never receive a note; a collection order was being offered to couriers.
- A KV key was written as UTF-8 and read back as Latin-1.
- Choosing euros blanked the storefront; a fourth copy of money formatting drew 1500 lek as $15.00;
  one formatter now serves every surface.
- A Content Security Policy header dropped most of every stylesheet; styles now live in files the
  policy allows, and every mask icon draws again.
- The room: tips without a till, refunded tips, refund currency, rounds without a phone, and the
  payment method at pay time, all found in a live walk and fixed.

## Security and privacy

- The 2026-09-21 red-team pass deleted two unauthenticated write routes, closed three route
  families, and bound the live socket to its venue (commit `727bc591`).
- Thirty-seven owner routes authorised one venue and acted on another; each now resolves the venue
  once, and `tools/gates/one-venue.sh` refuses the pattern.
- An order is no longer readable by anyone who knows its id: reading needs the guest's key.
- Forgetting a customer redacts in place and declares it with a `Forgotten` event, so the log's hash
  chain still verifies.
- Nightly copies are rotated: every copy for 7 days, then weekly ones to 21 days.
- A sealing path for the nightly copies exists and is off until the operator sets `BACKUP_SEAL_PK`.
  dowiz makes no claim of post-quantum encryption. The kernel's ML-DSA-65 signature implementation is
  verified byte-exact against NIST ACVP vectors and is not on the live request path.

## Infrastructure

- **No SQL.** D1 was removed: every venue's records are bebop images in its own Durable Object, and
  `tools/gates/no-sql.sh` holds prepared statements at zero.
- **One image per handler, one clock read per request**, each held at zero by a gate.
- **Gates**, each shipped with a proof that it can fire: file size, float money, clock, one image,
  one venue, no scoring, consent, idempotency, vocabulary, paths, unreached capabilities and more;
  `tools/gates/run-all.sh` runs them all and prints one table.
- **Conservation audit** (`e2e/gates/conservation.mjs`): the laws that the register adds up, run
  against a live venue.
- **Per-role live walks** (`e2e/walk/`) for guest, owner, waiter, kitchen and courier.
- **bebop-wasm**: the image reader in wasm32 and the four-reader parity gate (native, wasm32,
  Python oracle, bebop on AArch64).
- **The bebop language** (`bebop-lang/`): two months of compiler, checker and verification work,
  recorded in its own roadmap.
- **CI**: a new `ci.yml` over the crates the live product is made of, the gates, the browser tests
  and a wasm32 build; production probes every 15 minutes (`health-cron.yml`); the per-role walks
  daily against a QA host only (`key-flows.yml`); weekly mutation testing; Dependabot; releases
  published from tags (`release.yml`).
- **Documentation**: a rewritten README, `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/testing.md`,
  `docs/operations.md`, `docs/code-quality.md`, and a wiki under `docs/wiki/` with a guide per role.

## Known limitations

- An order past `PENDING` ends only through a refund; `DELIVERED` and `PICKED_UP` are final.
- There is no kitchen display; the kitchen hears about orders through Telegram, the printer or the
  console.
- The stock ledger does nothing on a venue until it enters supplies and recipes.
- No product crate is `cargo fmt`-clean yet; see `docs/code-quality.md`.
- The fixes listed as Wave G in
  [`docs/design/AUDIT-BUGS-BLINDSPOTS-TESTS-2026-09-24.md`](../design/AUDIT-BUGS-BLINDSPOTS-TESTS-2026-09-24.md)
  are open unless the tree says otherwise.

## Upgrading

There is nothing to migrate: the Worker is deployed from `workers/api` with `wrangler deploy` (see
[`docs/operations.md`](../operations.md)).
